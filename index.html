The Exhibits
№ 01 · the one I'm trying to make money with
PaperTrail Write
Monitored Writing · SaaS

A browser-based, monitored writing surface. Tracks time-on-task, paste events, tab-switching, and whether pasted text gets quietly deleted afterwards. Does not log keystrokes, clipboard contents, or anything off-page. The teacher gets a session report; the student gets a clean place to write. Lifetime revenue to date: $4.99. We are, technically, profitable on a per-bagel basis.

REV: $4.99 · YTD ↗ № 02
PaperTrail (Extension)
Chrome Extension

The Chrome companion. Captures writing as it happens inside Google Docs, so authorship is something you can show — not something you have to swear to. Turnitin tells you if it was copied. This tells you if it was theirs.

Classroom Games
Hub · AP Lang · TOK · EE · Research

The main hangar. Hexagonal Thinking, Evidence Hunt, the IB Dashboard, TOK lessons (maps, paradigm shifts, power and knowledge), thesis crafting, the EE formatting game, IELTS practice — about two dozen tools in one place. If only one link works on this page, make it this one.

↗ № 04
ASD English Dept.
Department Hub

The official-ish homepage of the English department. Curricula, resources, links the team actually uses. Built because the alternative was another shared Google folder.

↗ № 05
Dept. Library
Books · By Classroom

A directory of which books live in which classrooms. Not a checkout system — just a map. If you need Gatsby, it tells you whose room to walk to. Apps Script: the duct tape of education.

↗ № 06
AI Integrity Report
Research · Turnitin Study

An analysis of Turnitin AI-detection data from amnesty programs in AP Lang and IB Extended Essays, including a 44-document validation study. Available in administrator and parent-facing flavours. The "innovative grading approach" that the wood block is, in part, blaming.

↗ № 07
Agent Smith
Personal Investing Project

A long-running personal investment project, anchored in passive index philosophy and named after the only Smith allowed to be in two places at once. Not an app you can use. Not financial advice. Definitely not bullet-time.
